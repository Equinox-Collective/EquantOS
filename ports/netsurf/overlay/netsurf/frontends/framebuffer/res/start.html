The address bar takes addresses (youtube.com) and search words. YouTube and GitHub are shown through site scripts (/usr/share/netsurf/sitejs): they turn the data those sites embed in their pages into markup NetSurf can lay out, and videos play in the built-in player. Add ?ns_raw=1 to an address to see the page as the site sent it.
